What services can I find on the Uber Handyman app?

You can access a variety of services like plumbing, electrical work, carpentry, and home repairs, all through the Uber Handyman app developed by Code Regime Technologies.

How does the Uber Handyman app ensure reliable professionals?

Uber Handyman app ensures reliable professionals through a thorough vetting process, background checks, skill verification, and a rating system that allows customers to review past performance.

Is the Uber Handyman app customizable for my business needs?

Yes, the Uber Handyman app offers business customization options including branded interfaces, service catalog customization, and integration capabilities with your existing business systems.

Can I track the handyman's arrival in real time?

Yes, the app features real-time GPS tracking that allows you to monitor your handyman's location and estimated time of arrival, similar to how you can track drivers in the regular Uber app.

How do I handle payments on the Uber Handyman app?

Payments are handled securely through the app with multiple options including credit/debit cards, digital wallets, and in some regions, cash payments. All transactions are receipted and recorded for your convenience.
