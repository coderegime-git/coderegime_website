What is a clone script, and is it the right choice for my business?

A clone script is a pre-built, fully functional platform that replicates the core features of a market leader — think Uber or UberEats — and comes with 100% source code you own outright. It makes sense when speed-to-market matters and you want to validate a business model without spending 12–18 months on a custom build. Our clone scripts are independently engineered, fully customisable, and white-labelled under your brand.

Do I get the full source code when I buy a clone script or commission a custom build?

Always. Whether you buy a clone script or commission a bespoke app, you walk away with complete, unencrypted source code after final payment — no licensing fees, no black-box dependencies, no strings attached. Your team can maintain, extend, or migrate the codebase freely. We're not in the business of manufactured dependency.

What is CabRegime, and how quickly can I launch a ride-hailing business with it?

CabRegime is our enterprise-grade ride-hailing platform — a fully engineered Uber alternative with passenger and driver apps, real-time dispatch, surge pricing, in-app payments, and a complete admin dashboard. Most clients finish white-labelling, branding, and deployment within 2–4 weeks of purchase. It's built for entrepreneurs, taxi operators, and fleet managers who want a production-ready product without building from the ground up.

What is FoodRegime, and does it support multi-restaurant operations?

FoodRegime is our food delivery and restaurant management platform built along the same lines as UberEats or DoorDash — covering customer ordering, restaurant management, and delivery partner tracking with real-time GPS. Yes, multi-restaurant operations are fully supported, with a single admin panel handling commissions, payouts, and analytics. It works equally well for city-wide aggregators, dark kitchen operators, and enterprise restaurant chains.

What is HandymanRegime, and what types of home services can it support?

HandymanRegime is our on-demand home services marketplace that connects customers with verified professionals for plumbing, electrical work, cleaning, carpentry, painting, appliance repair, and more. How long does it typically take to develop a custom mobile or web application?

A focused MVP generally runs 8–14 weeks. A full-featured business application with multiple user roles and integrations typically takes 4–6 months. Enterprise systems with more complex workflows fall on a 6–12 month horizon. After the initial discovery session, we put together a detailed project roadmap with clear milestone dates — so you know what's coming before a single line of code is written.
