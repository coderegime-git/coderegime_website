(Daily Wellness Check-in App)

WELL-CHECK

Project Overview

Well-Check is an application designed to manage customer relationships with ease and automate processes for better efficiency. It offers peace of mind for people who live alone by providing a simple, reliable routine: a quick daily check-in that confirms everything’s okay—or triggers help fast when it isn’t.

The Problem

Millions Live Alone – But No One Should Feel Unseen
Every day, millions of seniors, students, and professionals live alone-and their loved ones worry in silence. Without a reliable wellness system, missed medications, unnoticed falls, and medical events can go undetected. Current solutions only respond after something goes wrong. It’s time for a smarter, proactive approach to mind.

The Solution

  • Users receive scheduled check-in prompts: Gentle daily wellness check messages ensure regular engagement.
  • Escalating prompts if no response: Smart system increases urgency with follow-ups to confirm safety.
  • Emergency contacts alerted, then authorities if needed: Automatic alerts protect users when silence persists.
  • Optional medication reminders: Timely, customizable notifications help maintain consistent routines.
  • Peace of mind with minimal intrusion: Subtle monitoring that respects privacy while keeping loved ones reassured.

How Well-Check Works

  • Step 1: You pick a check-in time (or times) that fit your day.
  • Step 2: The app sends a gentle notification requiring a quick response: “I’m okay” or “I need help.”
  • Step 3: If you tap Help, Well-Check immediately alerts your chosen contact(s) with your status and guidance.
  • Step 4: If you don’t respond within a short grace window, the app escalates automatically —notifying your contact that you missed your check-in and may need follow-up.

Whether it’s acknowledging a check-in prompt, sending an alert to emergency contacts, or marking a task complete, Well-Check makes it all possible with a single tap. By minimizing steps and maximizing ease of use, the app provides instant peace of mind while maintaining an intuitive, user-friendly experience.
